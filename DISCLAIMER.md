Samar is a paper-trading practice tool with an optional live path that is off by default. It is not a broker, not an adviser, and not investment advice. You can lose money. The live path is experimental.

Simulated orders fill inside Samar. They do not leave the machine. A Public.com key can also place orders, and only from `apps/api/src/samar_api/live/`, after you type the confirmation on the Live page. Until then the key is used to read the account.

You bring your own AI key and your own brokerage key. Samar does not pay for model calls. Keys are encrypted before they are stored, and no API response includes the key.

Market history in the demo is a frozen personal pull of daily bars. It is not a licensed market-data feed. Do not share another person's brokerage data through this app.
